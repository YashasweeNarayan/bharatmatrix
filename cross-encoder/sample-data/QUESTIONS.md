## 11_kaveri_textiles_q2_results

**Q1.** What was Kaveri Textiles' revenue from operations in Q2 FY27?  
**Answer:** ₹1,284.6 crore, compared with ₹1,102.4 crore in Q2 FY26.  

**Q2.** What profit after tax did Kaveri Textiles report for Q2 FY27?  
**Answer:** ₹107.5 crore (basic EPS ₹10.75).  

**Q3.** What is the interim dividend per share and the record date?  
**Answer:** ₹2.50 per equity share (25% of face value), record date 14 November 2026.  

**Q4.** How much of Q2 FY27 revenue came from exports?  
**Answer:** 41% of revenue from operations.  

**Q5.** What was Kaveri Textiles' net debt at 30 September 2026?  
**Answer:** ₹1,084 crore, down from ₹1,162 crore at 31 March 2026.  

**Q6.** Which segment had the highest revenue in Q2 FY27?  
**Answer:** Yarn, with ₹612.4 crore.  

**Q7.** What was the first-half capital expenditure and what was it for?  
**Answer:** ₹96 crore, mostly for a spinning line expansion at the Erode unit adding 38,000 spindles.  

**Q8.** What were the finance costs in Q2 FY27?  
**Answer:** ₹41.2 crore, down from ₹47.8 crore a year earlier.  

**Q9.** What kind of audit opinion covered these results?  
**Answer:** A limited review by the statutory auditors with an unmodified conclusion.  


## 12_himalaya_housing_finance_annual_report

**Q10.** What were Himalaya Housing Finance's assets under management at 31 March 2026?  
**Answer:** ₹18,420 crore, up 21% from ₹15,223 crore.  

**Q11.** What was the net interest margin and cost-to-income ratio?  
**Answer:** Net interest margin 4.1% on average assets; cost-to-income ratio 31.4%.  

**Q12.** What was the profit after tax and the recommended dividend?  
**Answer:** ₹486 crore, up 24% from ₹392 crore; dividend of ₹6 per equity share.  

**Q13.** What were gross and net NPAs as at 31 March 2026?  
**Answer:** Gross NPA 1.38% and net NPA 0.71%.  

**Q14.** How much ECL provision was held against Stage 2 assets?  
**Answer:** ₹120 crore against ₹952 crore of Stage 2 loan assets.  

**Q15.** What was the provision coverage ratio on Stage 3 assets?  
**Answer:** 49.6%.  

**Q16.** What was the capital adequacy ratio versus the regulatory minimum?  
**Answer:** CRAR 24.8% against a 15% minimum, of which Tier I was 23.9%.  

**Q17.** What share of borrowings came from bank term loans?  
**Answer:** 46% of total borrowings.  

**Q18.** What was the weighted average cost of borrowings?  
**Answer:** 7.82% during the year.  

**Q19.** What geographic concentration risk does the company disclose?  
**Answer:** Himachal Pradesh and Uttarakhand together account for 38% of AUM, so a regional slowdown or disaster could affect collections.  


## 13_deccan_power_rating_rationale

**Q20.** What is the new rating on Deccan Power's NCD programme and what was it before?  
**Answer:** AA-/Stable, upgraded from A+/Positive, on a ₹2,500 crore NCD programme.  

**Q21.** What is Deccan Power's installed capacity and its mix?  
**Answer:** 3,200 MW: 1,800 MW thermal, 900 MW solar and 500 MW wind; renewables are 44%.  

**Q22.** What share of revenue comes from state distribution companies?  
**Answer:** About 62% from three state distribution companies, a counterparty concentration risk.  

**Q23.** How did receivable days change and why?  
**Answer:** Improved to 96 days from 124 days after state companies cleared old dues under a late payment surcharge scheme.  

**Q24.** What were FY2025-26 EBITDA and EBITDA margin?  
**Answer:** EBITDA ₹3,180 crore, a 42.5% margin on ₹7,480 crore revenue.  

**Q25.** What was net debt to EBITDA and the DSCR?  
**Answer:** Net debt to EBITDA 3.6x; DSCR 1.52x.  

**Q26.** What is the planned capex and how is it funded?  
**Answer:** ₹4,800 crore over FY2026-27 to FY2028-29, funded 70:30 debt to equity.  

**Q27.** What could trigger a downgrade of the rating?  
**Answer:** DSCR falling below 1.25x or receivable days rising above 120 days.  

**Q28.** What could trigger an upgrade of the rating?  
**Answer:** Net debt to EBITDA falling below 3.0x on a sustained basis.  

**Q29.** What was the thermal plant load factor?  
**Answer:** 74% in FY2025-26.  


## 14_sagar_pharma_qip_term_sheet

**Q30.** What is the size of Sagar Pharmaceuticals' QIP?  
**Answer:** Up to ₹1,200 crore.  

**Q31.** What is the QIP floor price and the maximum discount?  
**Answer:** ₹1,462.50 per equity share, with up to a 5% discount permitted.  

**Q32.** What is the minimum issue price after the full discount?  
**Answer:** ₹1,389.38 per equity share.  

**Q33.** How many shares would be issued at the floor price and what dilution is that?  
**Answer:** About 82.05 lakh shares, roughly 3.4% of post-issue paid-up capital.  

**Q34.** How would promoter shareholding change?  
**Answer:** From 54.2% to about 52.4%, since promoters are not participating.  

**Q35.** How much of the proceeds is for debt repayment?  
**Answer:** ₹620 crore for repayment or prepayment of borrowings.  

**Q36.** What will the new API plant spending be and where is the plant?  
**Answer:** ₹380 crore for a new API plant at Visakhapatnam.  

**Q37.** What are the minimum allottees and maximum single-allottee share for this QIP?  
**Answer:** At least five allottees (issue above ₹250 crore), and no allottee may get more than 50%.  

**Q38.** What is the lock-in on shares allotted in the QIP?  
**Answer:** One year from allotment, except sales on a recognised stock exchange.  

**Q39.** What regulatory risk does Sagar Pharmaceuticals disclose?  
**Answer:** A USFDA Form 483 with four observations at the Baddi plant (August 2026), which could delay US approvals.

## cross-document

**X1.** Does Kaveri Textiles report the same Q2 FY27 revenue in its results filing and its investor presentation?  
**Answer:** No. The results filing shows ₹1,284.6 crore; the investor presentation says ₹1,248.6 crore (digits transposed). Neither document acknowledges or reconciles it. A correct system should flag it (the presentation's 16.5% growth matches ₹1,284.6 crore).  

**X2.** Is Kaveri's EBITDA margin in the presentation consistent with its reported results?  
**Answer:** Yes, it is consistent. EBITDA excluding other income = PBT 143.6 + finance costs 41.2 + depreciation 52.4 - other income 18.3 = ₹218.9 crore; 218.9 / 1,284.6 = 17.0%. It only matches the results filing's revenue, not the presentation's mistyped ₹1,248.6 crore. Good negative control for the contradiction detector.
