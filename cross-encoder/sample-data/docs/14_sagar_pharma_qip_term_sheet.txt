Qualified Institutions Placement: Summary of Key Terms

This summary describes a fictional qualified institutions placement under Chapter VI of the SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018, created for software testing. It is not an offer of securities.

Promoter shareholding would decrease from 54.2% before the issue to about 52.4% after the issue, because promoters are not participating.

Investor eligibility and lock-in

Allotment is made only to qualified institutional buyers. If the issue size is more than ₹250 crore, there must be at least five allottees, and no single allottee may receive more than 50% of the issue.

The equity shares allotted cannot be sold for one year from the date of allotment, except on a recognised stock exchange.
