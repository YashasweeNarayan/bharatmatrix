Himalaya Housing Finance Limited

Annual Report FY2025-26: Management Discussion and Financial Highlights

Himalaya Housing Finance Limited is a fictional housing finance company created for software testing. All amounts are in ₹ crore unless stated otherwise.

Business performance

Assets under management grew 21% to ₹18,420 crore as at 31 March 2026, from ₹15,223 crore a year earlier.

Disbursements during FY2025-26 were ₹6,240 crore, and the company served 1,42,000 active borrowers through 214 branches across 11 states.

Net interest income was ₹812 crore and the net interest margin was 4.1% on average assets; the cost-to-income ratio was 31.4%.

Profit after tax rose 24% to ₹486 crore from ₹392 crore in FY2024-25, and the Board recommended a dividend of ₹6 per equity share.

Asset quality and Ind AS 109 provisioning

Gross non-performing assets (Stage 3) were 1.38% of loans and net non-performing assets were 0.71% as at 31 March 2026.

Capital and funding

The capital adequacy ratio (CRAR) was 24.8% against the regulatory minimum of 15%, of which Tier I capital was 23.9%.

Risk factors

Himachal Pradesh and Uttarakhand together account for 38% of assets under management, so a regional slowdown or a natural disaster in these hill states could affect collections.
