Kaveri Textiles Limited

Investor Presentation: Q2 FY27 Highlights

Kaveri Textiles Limited is a fictional company created for software testing. All amounts are in ₹ crore unless stated otherwise.

Quarter highlights

Revenue from operations was ₹1,248.6 crore in Q2 FY27, up 16.5% year on year.

EBITDA excluding other income was ₹218.9 crore, a margin of 17.0%.

The order book stands at ₹2,150 crore, which provides visibility for the next two quarters.

Return on capital employed (ROCE) was 19.3% on an annualised basis.

Outlook and sustainability

Management guides for revenue growth of 14% to 16% in FY2026-27 and planned capital expenditure of ₹260 crore for the full year.

Renewable energy met 28% of power needs in Q2 FY27, and the company targets 50% by FY2029-30.
