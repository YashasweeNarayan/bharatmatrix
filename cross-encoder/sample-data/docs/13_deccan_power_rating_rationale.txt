Arvalli Credit Ratings (fictional agency)

Rating Rationale: Deccan Power and Infra Limited

Arvalli Credit Ratings has upgraded the rating on the ₹2,500 crore non-convertible debenture programme of Deccan Power and Infra Limited to AA-/Stable from A+/Positive. Both the agency and the company are fictional and were created for software testing.

Key rating drivers

Installed capacity is 3,200 MW, made up of 1,800 MW of thermal, 900 MW of solar and 500 MW of wind capacity, so renewables account for 44% of capacity.

The thermal plant load factor was 74% in FY2025-26.

About 62% of revenue comes from three state distribution companies, which concentrates counterparty risk.

Receivable days improved to 96 days from 124 days a year earlier after the state companies cleared old dues under a late payment surcharge scheme.

A capital expenditure programme of ₹4,800 crore is planned over FY2026-27 to FY2028-29, to be funded in a 70:30 debt to equity ratio.

Rating sensitivities

The rating could be upgraded if net debt to EBITDA falls below 3.0x on a sustained basis.

The rating could be downgraded if the DSCR falls below 1.25x or if receivable days rise above 120 days.
