Notes

The Board of Directors declared an interim dividend of ₹2.50 per equity share (25% of face value) for FY2026-27, with a record date of 14 November 2026.

Exports contributed 41% of revenue from operations in Q2 FY27, mainly to the United States and the European Union.

Net debt stood at ₹1,084 crore as at 30 September 2026, compared with ₹1,162 crore as at 31 March 2026.

Capital expenditure in the first half was ₹96 crore, mostly for a spinning line expansion at the Erode unit that adds 38,000 spindles.

The statutory auditors carried out a limited review of these results and expressed an unmodified conclusion.
